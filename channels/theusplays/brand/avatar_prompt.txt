Design a professional, high-quality logo mark for a YouTube gameplay channel: a symbol
only, with no wordmark. The mark is built on the capital letter "{initials}", the channel's
initial. The channel makes videos about big games — secrets, tests, guides, details —
without story spoilers, and will cover several games, so the mark must not reference any
specific game or console brand. It will be used as the YouTube channel icon.

THE MARK:
A capital "{initials}" built from the directional pad of a game controller: take the
plus-shaped D-pad cross and remove its top arm, so the left, right and bottom arms form a
heavy, geometric "{initials}". Keep the D-pad cues: squared arm ends with slightly rounded
corners, a shallow circular dimple at the centre where the arms meet, and a small
triangular arrow notch pressed into the end of each of the three arms. It must read first
as a bold letter and second as a D-pad. The letter is vivid yellow and sits inside one
thick, plain yellow circular ring with an even gap around it, on a flat near-black
({background}) background with no texture.

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS:
- The image contains exactly one letterform, the "{initials}". Do NOT write the channel
  name, any word, any other letter or any number anywhere: nothing under, over or beside
  the mark.
- Square 1:1 composition, the mark centred both ways with a generous margin on every
  side (the icon is cropped to a circle), works at 800x800px minimum
- Exactly one accent colour: vivid lemon yellow ({accent}), plus near-black ({background}).
  Nothing else is coloured.
- Do not imitate Grand Theft Auto, Rockstar Games, PlayStation, Xbox or Nintendo: never a
  letter inside a yellow square or rounded square, no star, no Pricedown-style lettering,
  no triangle-circle-cross-square button symbols, no pink or orange sunset, no palm trees
- No map, no location pin, no route, no crosshair, no gun sight, no target reticle
- Clean edges, high contrast — must be legible at 88x88px and still read at 24x24px
- Professional logo quality, as if drawn by a senior brand designer at a creative agency
- Flat colour only: no gradients, no bevel, no 3D, no drop shadow, no glow, no lens flare

Output: the mark centred on its square background. No mockups, no phone screens,
no merchandise, no presentation boards, no text. Just the clean mark.
