Design a professional, high-quality logo mark for a YouTube gameplay channel: a symbol
only, with no wordmark. The mark is built on the capital letter "{initials}", the channel's
initial. The channel goes into open-world games to check things out for the viewer —
hidden places, secrets, "can you actually do this?" tests and quick guides, always
without story spoilers. It will cover several
games over time, so the mark must not reference any specific game. It will be used as
the YouTube channel icon (square, shown cropped to a circle).

THE MARK:
A heavy, geometric capital "{initials}" with a thick, squared-off crossbar. Its stem tapers
down into the point of a map location pin, with a small round hole near the tip like a
real pin, so the mark reads both as the letter and as a map pin. The letter sits
inside one thick, plain circular ring that opens at the bottom where the pin point
passes through it. The ring carries the silhouette when the icon is shrunk to 24 pixels.

The ring is plain: no ticks, no notches, no crosshair lines, nothing at the 12, 3 or 9
o'clock positions. It must never read as a gun sight, scope or target reticle.

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS:
- The image contains exactly one letterform, the "{initials}". Do NOT write the channel
  name, any word, any other letter or any number anywhere: nothing under, over or beside
  the mark.
- Square 1:1 composition, the mark centred both ways with a generous margin on every
  side (the icon is cropped to a circle), works at 800x800px minimum
- Flat near-black background ({background}), no texture, no vignette
- Exactly one accent colour: vivid lemon yellow ({accent}). Nothing else is coloured.
- Do not imitate Grand Theft Auto or Rockstar Games: never put the letter inside a filled
  yellow square or rounded square, no star, no Pricedown-style lettering, no pink or
  orange sunset gradient, no palm trees
- Clean edges, high contrast — must be legible at 88x88px and still read at 24x24px
- Professional logo quality, as if drawn by a senior brand designer at a creative agency
- Flat colour only: no gradients, no bevel, no 3D, no drop shadow, no glow, no lens flare

Output: the mark centred on its dark square background. No mockups, no phone screens,
no merchandise, no presentation boards, no text. Just the clean mark on its dark background.
