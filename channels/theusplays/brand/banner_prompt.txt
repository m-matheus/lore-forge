A very wide, very dark, flat graphic background for a gaming channel banner. Near-black
({background}) with a subtle fine grain. Energetic but clean, like a modern esports
broadcast graphic: bold diagonal stripes and sharp angular slashes of vivid lemon yellow
({accent}) on the far left and the far right of the image, with a few thin parallel
diagonal yellow lines and a faint dark-charcoal halftone dot gradient around them.

Layout matters, because on computers only a thin horizontal strip through the vertical
middle of the banner is shown: the yellow diagonal shapes on each side must cross the
vertical middle of the image (from 35% to 65% of the height) so they are visible in that
strip. They stay inside the outer 18% of the width on each side.

Across the whole background, including the centre, add one faint texture in dark
charcoal grey and low contrast: a scattered pattern of small plus-shaped game controller
D-pad crosses, at a few small sizes and slight rotations, spread evenly like confetti.
White text will be placed in the centre later and must stay perfectly readable, so the
centre has no shapes other than that faint texture.

Modern, minimal, vector-like; the only colour is the yellow, and the texture is not
yellow. No map, no route, no pin. No game references beyond the D-pad crosses: no
controllers, no characters, no weapons, no cars, no city. Do not imitate Grand Theft Auto
or Rockstar Games: no pink or orange sunset, no palm trees, no neon, no star.

ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no borders.
