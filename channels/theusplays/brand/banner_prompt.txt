A very wide, very dark, flat graphic background for a gaming channel banner: a stylized
top-down map at night. Fine topographic contour lines and a faint street grid in dark
charcoal grey, barely visible against near-black ({background}).

One vivid lemon yellow ({accent}) dashed route line crosses the whole image from left to
right THROUGH THE VERTICAL MIDDLE, because on computers only a thin horizontal strip
through the middle of the banner is shown:
- it enters at the far left edge at about half the image height,
- dips gently to about 60% of the height while it passes under the empty centre,
- rises again and ends at a small yellow location pin near the far right edge, at about
  half the image height,
- with a few tiny yellow waypoint dots along it, mostly in the left and right thirds.
Nothing else is yellow, and the top and bottom of the image are only faint map.

The CENTRE must stay almost empty and dark: the middle 60% of the width, from 35% to 57%
of the height, is a calm, plain expanse of map where text will be placed later. The route
passes below that area, never through it.

Modern, minimal, vector-like; the only colour is the yellow. Do not imitate Grand Theft
Auto or Rockstar Games: no pink or orange sunset, no palm trees, no neon, no star, no
recognizable city or coastline.

ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no borders.
