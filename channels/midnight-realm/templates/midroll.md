# Mid-roll break (~40 seconds, ~80 words)

Written as the first paragraph of the body section it lands in, before that section's
chapter announcement. It happens exactly once in the video and is never referred to
again.

The one moment the narrator steps out of the story. It stays as quiet as the rest: no
energy change, no "hey guys", nothing that would wake someone.

1. **Acknowledge both listeners.** Some are still wide awake and following closely,
   some are drifting in and out. Both are fine. One sentence.
2. **Thanks for being here.** One sentence, plain.
3. **The ask.** Subscribing, and a comment — the character, place or game they would
   like to hear about on another night. Two sentences at most, no urgency, no reason
   given that flatters the channel.
4. **Back in.** Two or three words that return to the story, then the chapter
   announcement follows immediately.

Never mention the algorithm, the bell, watch time, "smashing" anything, or how long the
video took to make. Never ask for a like twice, and never ask anyone to stay awake.
